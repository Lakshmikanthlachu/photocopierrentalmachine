Service details

Managed Print Services

One contract for every device in every office — monitored, supplied and serviced by us, with live analytics that cut print spend by up to 30%.

Overview

Most offices run a patchwork of printers from different vendors, each with its own toner, contract and support number. Managed Print Services (MPS) replaces all of that with a single partner who owns the outcome: devices that work and a print bill that keeps going down.

We start with a free audit of every device you own or lease, then design a right-sized fleet, take over support for existing machines and replace the worst performers with new Copixa devices. Every machine reports into your live portal from day one.

Up to 30% lower spend

Right-sizing, rules-based printing and cheaper cost per page.

99%+ fleet uptime

Predictive maintenance fixes faults before users notice.

Zero supply admin

Toner and parts ship automatically based on live levels.

Audit-ready security

Secure release, encrypted drives and full user logs.
