Measuring the carbon footprint of your print fleet

Energy per page, recycled toner and end-of-life take-back: how to report print emissions honestly.

Daniel Okafor

Field Service Lead · Sep 23, 2026

Print is a small but visible part of most companies' emissions, and it is easy to report badly. This is how we help clients measure the footprint of their fleet honestly.

Start with energy per page

Each device reports its energy use and page counts, which gives a real energy-per-page figure rather than a manufacturer estimate. Older devices often use three times more energy per page than a current model.

Paper, toner and transport

  • Paper is usually the largest share, so duplex defaults matter most
  • Remanufactured toner cuts cartridge emissions by around half
  • Consolidated deliveries reduce van miles for supplies
The greenest page is the one you never print, and the next greenest is printed on a modern, well-maintained device

End of life

When a device leaves a client office we refurbish it for another contract or recycle it through certified partners. Every client receives an annual report with energy, paper, toner and end-of-life figures for their ESG pack.
